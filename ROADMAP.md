# Public roadmap

Debt World grows through verified user needs, safety review, and privacy-preserving aggregate signals—not by exposing individual financial data.

## Now: staged public beta

- Observe onboarding completion, return visits, feedback, moderation load, and operating cost.
- Improve mobile, slow-network, keyboard, screen-reader, and multilingual usability.
- Keep exact financial data private while expanding reviewed anonymous stories.
- Strengthen backup, recovery drills, incident response, and region-specific legal review.

## Next

- Pet growth driven by confirmed repayment progress, with no punitive streaks.
- Forum-like topics, one-level comments, fixed reactions, reporting, and moderation.
- More useful aggregate world data only after privacy thresholds are met.
- New debt communities discovered from repeated, consented, anonymous labels.
- Additional languages opened in cohorts with human review of privacy and crisis wording.

## Later

- Mobile app or mini-program only after the web product reaches meaningful active use.
- Regional infrastructure for markets that require local hosting, filing, or data arrangements.
- Carefully reviewed educational calculators and links to qualified local help.

This roadmap is directional. Safety, privacy, and real-user evidence take priority over feature count.
