## 1. Product launch / 产品更新

**Title:** I turned Debt World into a 3D city you can walk and fly through

I am building Debt World, a small product for making debt pressure easier to understand. Its new public beta is a 3D cyber city: walk, fly, inspect financial districts, and decide whether the underlying repayment planner is useful before signing up.

The city is a way into the problem—not a way to pretend it disappeared. Flying never changes a balance. After sign-in, districts connect to existing debt records, cash flow, real repayment updates, and an anonymous community.

Try it without an account: https://www.debtworld.org/en/city?src=githublaunch

## 2. Practical repayment planning / 还款计划

**Title:** The city is the doorway. The repayment plan is the reason to return.

I do not want Debt World to be interesting for five minutes and useless afterward. Under the new 3D city is the practical part: multiple debt records, due dates, balances, APR, monthly payments, and updates after a real payment happens.

The repayment beacon visualizes recorded progress. It is not a bank connection and does not certify that a transaction occurred. You stay in control of what gets recorded.

If you try the beta, I would value one specific answer: which planning detail would make you come back next month?

Plan entry: https://www.debtworld.org/en?src=githubplanner

## 3. Cash-flow clarity / 现金流

**Title:** A balance tells one part of the story. The month tells another.

A total balance cannot show whether this month's plan leaves enough room for ordinary living. That is why Debt World has a cash-flow exchange: a place connected to the existing income, living-cost, and debt-payment view.

I am testing whether giving those facts a place in a city makes them easier to approach. It is not a promise that a simple budget solves every situation. Sometimes the useful outcome is noticing that a plan does not fit and identifying the next fact to check.

Explore: https://www.debtworld.org/en/city?src=githubcashflow

**中文版本：** 总欠款是一部分，这个月怎么过也是一部分。现金流枢纽连接收入、生活开销和月供，让这些数字能放在一起看。它不会假装“做个预算就什么都解决了”；有时先发现计划放不下，才能知道下一步要核对什么。试玩：https://www.debtworld.org/city?src=githubcashflow

## 4. Privacy and trust / 隐私边界

**Title:** A shared world does not need everyone's exact finances

I want Debt World to feel less isolating without turning people's distress into public data. The public 3D preview is scenery-only. In the signed-in world, exact personal amounts belong in the account owner's view, while shared avatars and separately approved stories use limited public information.

No bank is connected. The city has no claim to independently verified transactions. It is not a public debt ranking.

Please try the preview without entering personal information. Which sentence about privacy would you want clarified before trusting a continuing plan?

https://www.debtworld.org/en/city?src=githubprivacy

## 5. Founder story / 产品初衷

**Title:** I built Debt World for the lives that do not appear in the highlight reel

Social feeds can make ordinary financial pressure feel like evidence that you are the only person falling behind. That feeling is the reason I started Debt World.

The new 3D city gives mortgages, cards, student loans, cash flow, and repayment a visible place. The goal is not to compete over hardship. It is to make a situation easier to name, organize, and talk about without performing wealth or success.

The free preview needs no account. I am the founder, and I would value feedback on whether the experience feels supportive, distracting, or neither.

## 6. Thoughtful game design / 游戏化设计

**Title:** What should not be gamified in a debt product?

Building Debt World's 3D city has made one design boundary especially important: exploration and encouragement must not pretend to be financial progress.

Walking, flying, and visiting a district can make the product easier to approach. A recorded repayment is a different kind of event. The beacon reads that existing progress; it does not reward movement by reducing debt.

I am interested in thoughtful feedback from interaction and game designers: where does a visual world make a difficult task clearer, and where might it make the task feel trivialized?

https://www.debtworld.org/en/city?src=githubdesign

**中文版本：** 负债产品里，哪些事情不应该被游戏化？我给上岸星球划了一条线：飞行、探索和鼓励可以让人更愿意接近问题，但不能伪装成真实还款。想请交互和游戏设计的朋友看看：哪些设计让事情变清楚，哪些可能让严肃的压力被轻描淡写？https://www.debtworld.org/city?src=githubdesign

## Publication checklist

- Choose one angle suited to an actual audience. No unrelated-repository promotion or mass replies.
- Disclose the founder relationship; do not pose as a happy customer.
- Do not claim usage growth, debt-free results, universal device support, bank verification, or open-source availability.
- Use a real, privacy-safe screenshot only after reviewing it. No concept art described as product footage.
- GitHub Issue/comment, Reddit, or X publication needs the founder's per-task approval of the exact target and final content.
- Track actual visits, meaningful first use, and feedback when available. Do not count message variants, scenes, or decorative avatars as users.
