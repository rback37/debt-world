# A three-minute, no-private-data city walkthrough

[English city](https://www.debtworld.org/en/city?src=githubtest) · [中文城市](https://www.debtworld.org/city?src=githubtest)

You do not need an account, real debt, or a personal story to help.

1. Open the preview. Did the city load? If it did not, was the retry/fallback explanation understandable?
2. Try Walk and Fly. On a phone use the joystick; on a keyboard use WASD or arrows, with Q/E for flight altitude. Drag to look, or use the district directory.
3. Inspect one place. Did you understand what it would connect to after signing in?
4. Tell us one thing to improve. You may report that the city adds no value; that is useful feedback too.

Optional report:

- Broad device class and browser family (for example, Android phone / Chrome; no device identifiers).
- Action attempted.
- What you expected.
- What happened.
- Whether the result felt clearer, more stressful, or unchanged.

Do not include account names, passwords, exact personal finances, bank details, private conversations, or screenshots showing unrelated browser tabs or personal information.

Public product feedback: [existing beta discussion](https://github.com/rback37/debt-world/issues/1). Private feedback: the website's Feedback panel after sign-in.

## 中文

无需注册、填写债务或讲述私人经历。打开城市，试一下步行或飞行，从导航去一个街区，再告诉我们一个需要改善的地方。可以说“不觉得有用”，不需要好评。

反馈可以只写：大致设备与浏览器、尝试的动作、预期、实际结果，以及体验是更清楚、更焦虑还是没有变化。不要附账号、精确债务、收入、银行资料、私人对话或带有其他个人信息的截图。
