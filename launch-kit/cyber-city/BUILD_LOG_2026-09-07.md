# Debt World now has a city you can fly through

September 7, 2026 · Public beta update · Written from the founder's perspective

[Explore in English — no signup](https://www.debtworld.org/en/city?src=githubbuildlog) · [中文试玩](https://www.debtworld.org/city?src=githubbuildlog) · [Create a continuing plan](https://www.debtworld.org/en?src=githubbuildlog)

## Why a city?

I started Debt World because social feeds can make ordinary life feel like a personal failure. Everyone seems wealthy; the pressure behind the picture stays invisible.

The goal is not to build a debt leaderboard or turn distress into a game. It is to make a complicated situation easier to approach: understand the debts, see what is due, compare income with expenses, and find a realistic next step without having to perform success for strangers.

Today that idea has a new, explorable form: a real 3D cyber city.

## What changed

You can walk at street level, fly above the skyline, change the camera, inspect places, and travel directly to nine districts. The public scenery preview is available without an account in Chinese and English.

The districts have different jobs:

| Place | Connection in the signed-in product |
| --- | --- |
| Repayment beacon | Recorded repayment progress and existing payment tasks |
| Cash-flow exchange | Income, living costs, and monthly debt payments |
| Home quarter | Mortgage records |
| Credit arcade | Cards, instalments, and personal loans |
| Learning dome | Student loans |
| New-start works | Business, vehicle, medical, family/friend, and other debts |
| Echo plaza | Anonymous community stories |
| Starlight gardens | Encouragement and community support |
| Shore harbour | Progress and the next part of the journey |

Debt districts open an existing matching record, or the existing setup flow when none has been added. The repayment timeline still opens a confirmation form; it never records a payment merely because you visited a place.

Shared walkers, approved stories, and published community builds occupy an expanding outer promenade. The public preview has no simulated users. Buildings, decorative transport, and lights are scenery—not claims about real user numbers or money moving.

## Try it in three minutes

1. Open the public city preview. No account or personal numbers are needed.
2. Use **Walk** or **Fly**. WASD/arrows move, dragging changes the camera, and Q/E change altitude while flying. Touch controls and a district directory are also available.
3. Inspect a district. Does its purpose make sense before you sign in?
4. Only if useful, return to the main entry to try the existing local planning example or create an account for a continuing plan.

The lightweight classic map remains available in the signed-in world. Loading and performance will vary by device and network; older phones and WebGL failures need more real-world feedback.

## What has not changed

- No bank is connected. No money is transferred by the city.
- Repayment records are self-reported, not independently verified bank transactions.
- Other people's exact balances, income, and private AI conversations are not public city content.
- Public stories require separate consent and moderation.
- Kian is an educational organizing tool, not a financial adviser or lawyer, and does not promise debt-free outcomes.
- The public GitHub repository remains a product-and-community documentation home, not the production source repository. We are not announcing an open-source release.

## What I need feedback on

One honest observation is enough:

- Did the city make the next useful action clearer, or add unnecessary complexity?
- On your device, what was the first slow or confusing interaction?
- Could you tell which information is private, shared, or just scenery?
- Would a repayment plan give you a reason to return, independent of the city's appearance?

Use [the existing beta discussion](https://github.com/rback37/debt-world/issues/1) for public product feedback. Do not post account identifiers, passwords, exact financial amounts, bank details, private conversations, or another person's story. Private feedback belongs in the website's Feedback panel.

## 中文：从一张地图，到一座可以遨游的城市

我做上岸星球的初衷，是让普通人暂时离开“所有人都很有钱”的社交网络错觉。它不该让人比较谁欠得最多，而应该帮助人把压力拆开：欠在哪里、什么时候要还、每月剩多少、下一步需要先确认什么。

这次上线了可以步行、飞行、点选和传送的 3D 赛博城市。九个主题街区连接房贷、信用卡、学贷、经营与其他生活负担、现金流、真实还款和匿名社区。**无需注册就能先逛城市；需要长期保存自己的计划时，再创建账号。**

城市不会替你还款，飞行不会让余额变少，星光也不是金钱。公开试玩没有模拟用户，建筑与交通装饰不被当作真实用户数据。共享角色、审核通过的故事和玩家作品会进入更宽敞的外圈空间。

我最希望收到的不是一句“很酷”，而是：城市是否让你更容易找到下一步？哪一个按钮让你迷路？如果拿掉画面，这个还款计划本身是否仍值得回来使用？欢迎把不含隐私的体验反馈留在现有公测讨论里。

## Release evidence

The production deployment completed on September 7. Both language preview routes returned successful HTML with the 3D canvas. The implementation passed 13 automated checks covering rendering, core layout rules, and the isolated account/debt lifecycle. These checks are not a claim of universal device compatibility or fully automated graphical acceptance.
